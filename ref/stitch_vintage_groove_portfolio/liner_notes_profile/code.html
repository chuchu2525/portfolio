Liner Notes

The Articulate
Craftsman.

I don't just write software; I stitch it together. My approach to engineering is heavily influenced by the tactile world of high-quality craftsmanship. Just as a selvedge denim jacket develops character and molds to its wearer over years of use, robust architecture should be built to withstand the elements of scale and time.

The meticulous grooving of a vinyl record, the satisfying weight of heavy paper stock—these aren't just aesthetic preferences; they represent a philosophy of permanence. In an industry obsessed with the ephemeral, I build digital artifacts meant to endure.

Crafting durable code like a well-worn pair of jeans.
